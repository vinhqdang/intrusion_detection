# X2 equilibrium loss versus AUROC

Reference parameters (illustrative, see GameParams): V=1000, P=2000, lam=1, kM=100, kN=3000, pi=0.5, N_benign=1e+06, N_attack=50, L=1000, w=0.5, cB=2, novel_mode=worst, efficacy=True, attacker_query=score.

Primary: per seed, the equilibrium is solved on that seed's recall tables (the attacker knows the deployed detector). Each model's optimum is the lowest loss over its FPR and B (defender solves with that model only). Loss is reported as mean and bootstrap 95% interval over seeds. 'Loss-tied share' is the share of seeds in which the model is in the loss-tied set (within 1% of the lowest loss). AUROC: mean over families of the known-scenario AUROC; tied with the best when the paired difference over seeds has a 95% bootstrap interval containing 0 or |mean difference| < 0.0001. Secondary: the solve on seed-mean tables.
